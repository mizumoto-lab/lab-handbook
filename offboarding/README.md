# Leaving the lab

Last updated: 2026-08-05

Status: Under construction  

Next review: 2026-11

## 
Before leaving, each member should complete the relevant departure checklist. At minimum, the member and Nobu should:

- Confirm that all data, code, records, samples, and methods are accessible.
- Confirm that project repositories are owned by the lab organization.
- Document unfinished analyses, experiments, and maintenance responsibilities.
- Agree on a publication and authorship plan for unfinished work.
- Return lab property and transfer any necessary access.
- Remove personal or confidential information from shared systems.
- Identify how future questions and revisions will be handled.
