## Fieldwork checklist

### Together

- Review the field objective, itinerary, participants, transportation, and communication plan.
- Review weather, heat, wildlife, venomous insects, terrain, and other site-specific risks.
- Review emergency procedures and the location of first-aid supplies.
- Review collection permits and restrictions.
- Review the colony and specimen labeling system.
- Practice recording date, time, collector, locality, and decimal-degree GPS coordinates.
- Review sample transport, temporary storage, and return-to-lab procedures.
- Confirm responsibility for equipment before departure and after return.
- Confirm where field notes, coordinates, photographs, and collection records will be stored.

### Nobu

- Verify required driver, anaphylaxis, cutting-tool, and other field-safety training.
- Confirm permits, vehicle arrangements, emergency contacts, and required equipment.
- Confirm that fieldwork expectations are realistic given student's experience.

### Students

- Complete Driver’s Safety training (CR513E Auburn University Driver Training): https://ba.auburn.edu/rms/training/#drivertraining
- Complete ADPH Anaphylaxis Training through BioRaft: https://auburn.bioraft.com/rafttraining/course/878
- Watch the axe-use safety video: https://originaloutdoors.co.uk/article/axe-safety-basics-simple-rules-for-using-axe-safely-bushcraft-woodcraft/
  - Keep your body out of the direction of the swing.
  - Wear gloves when using cutting tools.
- Review the itinerary, risks, communication plan, and emergency procedures.
- Practice the specimen-labeling and field-record systems before departure.
- Confirm required clothing, personal supplies, and assigned equipment.
- Report any safety, accessibility, health, or travel concerns through an appropriate private channel.
